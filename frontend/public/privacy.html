Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

OfferPath ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use data through our Chrome Extension.

1. Data Collection

The OfferPath extension collects job application details (Company Name, Job Title, and URL) only when you explicitly click the "Save Application" button. We do not track your browsing history or collect any data in the background without your interaction.

2. Use of Data

The collected data is used exclusively to populate your personal job tracking dashboard. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

3. Authentication

We use secure tokens to sync your data between the extension and your OfferPath web account.

4. Permissions

The extension requires "Host Permissions" to detect job details on recruitment websites. This is used solely for the functionality mentioned above.

If you have any questions, please contact us via the project repository.
